1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ACADEMIC VALUE PROPOSITION
================================================================================
In humanitarian disaster response, two major systemic vulnerabilities persist:
  1. Financial opacity and misappropriation of disaster donations.
  2. Operational bottlenecks and fraud in donor receipt intake and resource allocation.

While blockchain technology (Ethereum/Sepolia Smart Contracts) resolves the first
vulnerability by providing an immutable, public, and auditable financial ledger, 
it does NOT solve data entry integrity (the "Garbage-In, Garbage-Out" problem).

By integrating Artificial Intelligence (Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing,
and Generative Decision Support), BBDRTS establishes a comprehensive Dual Trust Model:
  * AI handles the Intelligent Verification, Extraction, and Allocation before
    funds or records reach the blockchain.
  * Blockchain seals verified records into an immutable, decentralized ledger.

This dual architecture elevates the Capstone from a traditional Web3 CRUD dapp
into an enterprise-grade, defense-ready humanitarian relief platform.

3. MODULE A: AI DONATION RECEIPT & FRAUD INSPECTOR (OCR + VERIFICATION)
================================================================================
A. Problem Statement:
   Currently, donors who contribute via GCash, Maya, or direct Bank Transfer upload 
   a screenshot of their transaction slip into the system for manual NGO review.
   Panel Question: "What prevents a bad actor from uploading a blank image, a random 
   meme, a recycled receipt, or a photoshopped GCash screenshot claiming a ₱10,000 
   donation?"

B. How AI Solves It:
   When the donor attaches their receipt:
   1. Multi-Modal Vision Model (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash / Vision OCR) scans the image.
   2. Intelligent Information Extraction:
      - Transferred Amount (e.g., ₱1,500.00)
      - Reference Number (e.g., GCash: "1002 9841 8291", Maya: "REF-9812401")
      - Timestamp & Date of Transaction
      - Beneficiary Account Name / Number
      - Gateway Provider (GCash vs. Maya vs. BDO vs. BPI)
   3. Automated Cross-Check Rules:
      - Amount Match Check: Compares OCR extracted amount with donor's input.
      - Duplicate Reference Prevention: Scans database for matching Reference Nos.
      - Date Horizon Check: Confirms the receipt date is within 24-48 hours of filing.
   4. Admin & NGO Dashboard Inspection:
      - Displays AI Confidence Score (e.g., 96% Match).
      - Highlights extracted Reference Number with 1-click verify.
      - Flags suspicious mismatches in high-contrast red alerts before approval.

C. Prompt Engineering & API Contract:
   Engine: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash API (Multimodal JSON Mode)
   Prompt Template:
   """
   You are an automated financial auditor specializing in Philippine e-wallets
   (GCash, Maya) and Philippine Universal Bank slips (BDO, BPI, UnionBank, etc.).
   Analyze this receipt image and extract structured data in JSON:
   {
     "is_valid_receipt": boolean,
     "provider": "GCash" | "Maya" | "Bank" | "Unknown",
     "amount": number,
     "reference_number": string,
     "sender_name": string | null,
     "recipient_name": string | null,
     "transaction_date": string | null,
     "confidence_score": number (0.00 to 1.00),
     "tampering_signs": string | null
   }
   """

D. Offline Defense-Proof Fallback:
   If no API key is present or internet connectivity is disrupted during thesis demo:
   - Built-in heuristic regex scanner inspects base64 meta and regex patterns.
   - Graceful fallback: "Receipt staged for manual inspection (OCR bypass mode)".

4. MODULE B: AI RELIEF NEEDS ALLOCATOR & CAMPAIGN GENERATOR (DECISION SUPPORT)
================================================================================
A. Problem Statement:
   NGOs creating disaster campaigns often set arbitrary target amounts and percentages 
   for resource allocation (Food vs. Medical vs. Shelter vs. Logistics).
   Panel Question: "How do you ensure campaign budgets reflect realistic humanitarian 
   standards rather than inflated or unbalanced allocations?"

B. How AI Solves It:
   Inside OrganizationView ("Create Campaign" - Section 3 & 4):
   A new action button: "🤖 AI Smart Budget & Allocation Generator"
   1. Input Parameters:
      - Disaster Type: Typhoon, Flood, Earthquake, Landslide, Volcanic Eruption.
      - Severity Rating: Tropical Cyclone Warning Signal (Signal 1 to 5) / Intensity.
      - Target Beneficiaries: Number of affected families/individuals (e.g., 500 families).
      - Geographical Region: e.g., Southern Leyte (Visayas - coastal/island logistics).
   2. Intelligent Humanitarian Computation:
      - References NDRRMC (National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council)
        and UN Sphere Project humanitarian standards.
      - Automatically sets dynamic allocation sliders:
        * Typhoon Post-Disaster: 45% Food & Potable Water, 25% Emergency Shelter, 
          20% Medical Supplies, 10% First-Mile Transport Logistics.
        * Earthquake Post-Disaster: 35% Medical/Trauma Care, 35% Temporary Shelter, 
          20% Food Supplies, 10% Rescue Gear.
      - Calculates estimated unit relief packs (e.g., "₱1,250 covers 1 family for 7 days").
   3. AI Narrative & Mission Generator:
      - Produces an objective, high-clarity campaign title, description, and impact metrics
        ready for donor review.

C. Technical Implementation:
   - Client-side fast matrix fallback based on NDRRMC allocation tables.
   - LLM endpoint for customized, context-rich emergency narratives.

5. MODULE C: "BAYANIHAN AI" RELIEF COPILOT (WEB3 CONVERSATIONAL ASSISTANT)
================================================================================
A. Problem Statement:
   Everyday donors and disaster victims find Web3 concepts (Sepolia EVM, MetaMask, 
   gas fees, transaction hashes, smart contract escrow) intimidating and complex.
   Panel Question: "Is your blockchain system too complex for ordinary Filipino donors?"

B. How AI Solves It:
   "Bayanihan AI" is a sleek, floating Web3 assistant widget docked at the bottom-right 
   of the application (Landing, Donor, and Public views).
   1. Conversational Bilingual Support:
      - Fully fluent in Filipino/Tagalog and English.
      - Explains concepts in warm, accessible, compassionate language.
   2. Core Capabilities:
      - "Explain Transparency": Explains how smart contract escrow locks funds until 
        milestone requirements are satisfied by the NGO.
      - "Verify Transaction": Donor can paste a 0x hash; Bayanihan AI decodes it into 
        plain English ("Your 0.05 ETH donation was successfully received by Philippine Red Cross 
        for the Southern Leyte Typhoon Odette campaign").
      - "Smart Campaign Search": Directs donors based on urgency:
        * User: "Saan pinaka-kailangan ng gamot ngayon?"
        * AI: "Base sa verified campaigns natin, ang 'Cebu Flash Flood Medical Aid' ay 
          18% pa lang ang pondo. Pwede kang mag-donate via GCash o Crypto dito."
      - "Emergency Hotlines & Guidelines": Provides verified contact info for 
        NDRRMC, Red Cross 143, DSWD, and local CDRRMO.

C. Prompt & Grounding Architecture:
   - System Prompt enforces authoritative, empathetic, and anti-hallucination rules.
   - Grounded directly on the app's real-time active campaign data (`campaigns` array) 
     and accredited NGO registry (`ORGANIZATION` database).

8. THESIS DEFENSE STRATEGY & PANEL Q&A CHEATSHEET
================================================================================
Prepare these answers for common thesis panel critiques:

Q1: "Why did your team include AI if the primary thesis is Blockchain?"
A1: "Blockchain provides an infallible storage and transaction layer, but it cannot 
    judge the veracity of off-chain data submitted by humans. In humanitarian relief, 
    verifying fiat donation slips and calculating realistic disaster aid allocations 
    are real-world bottlenecks. AI acts as an intelligent ingestion guardrail, ensuring 
    that only validated, mathematically sound data is recorded on-chain."

Q2: "What if the AI hallucinates or misreads a low-quality GCash screenshot?"
A2: "We designed our AI as a Decision-Support System (Human-in-the-loop), not an 
    autonomous gatekeeper. The AI extracts details and assigns a confidence score. 
    If a receipt is blurry or suspicious, it flags the transaction for manual review 
    by NGO officials with highlighted discrepancies. The final approval always rests 
    with verified human administrators."

Q3: "What if there is no internet during a disaster?"
A3: "Our architecture follows a Graceful Degradation model:
    1. If online: Full Gemini Vision and Generative AI provides instant auto-checks.
    2. If offline: The system falls back to deterministic client-side NDRRMC allocation 
       matrices and traditional manual verification. The core blockchain and manual 
       relief workflows never fail even if AI services are temporarily unreachable."

Q4: "Did you train your own model?"
A4: "Training a custom foundational model from scratch requires millions of parameters 
    and is out of scope for a disaster management application. Instead, we implemented 
    advanced Multi-Modal Prompt Engineering, Zero-Shot Structured Data Extraction, 
    and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) grounded on live campaign context and 
    official Philippine NDRRMC humanitarian allocation guidelines."
