                         COMPREHENSIVE SYSTEM ENHANCEMENTS & FEATURE SUMMARY

This document provides a detailed summary of all technical implementations, architectural upgrades,
and production features integrated into the BBDRTS platform.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NOTIFICATION & ALERT CENTER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Real-Time In-App Notification & Alert Center

- Purpose:
  To provide immediate, event-driven visibility for Donors, Relief Organizations (NGOs), and
  System Administrators regarding mission-critical actions, eliminating the need to refresh or
  guess transactional and verification states.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * Persistent Drawer & Bell Widget:
    A dedicated notification bell in the global top header displays real-time unread badge counts.
    Clicking it smoothly opens an interactive side drawer with category filtering (All, Unread,
    Verification, Donations, Campaigns, Milestones).
  * Automated Lifecycle Trigger Events:
    - Donation Confirmed: Triggers immediate confirmation to the donor with transaction details
      and sends a real-time incoming fund notification to the beneficiary organization.
    - Institutional KYC Decision: Instantly alerts the NGO when their SEC Accreditation has been
      verified by the AI Document Vision Engine or reviewed by the Administrator.
    - Disaster Relief Deployment: Informs donors when relief goods or aid milestones have been
      dispatched to ground zero.
  * In-App Actions & Deep Linking:
    - "Mark as Read" and "Clear All" notification controls.
    - Deep-linking buttons inside each notification that navigate the user straight to the
      associated campaign, transaction ledger, or accreditation panel.
  * Resilient Backend Architecture:
    Backed by the `USER_NOTIFICATION` database table with fallback delivery mechanisms ensuring
    notifications survive across browser restarts and device switches.

2. PHILIPPINE DISASTER MAP & RADAR (DisasterRadarHeatmap)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Interactive Philippine Calamity Heatmap & Meteorological Doppler Radar

- Purpose:
  To visualize real-time geographical disaster density, calamity urgency, and relief fund
  distribution across all 17 administrative regions of the Philippines, enabling strategic aid
  dispatch and transparent donor decision-making.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * High-Precision Philippine Geo-Mapping:
    - Integrated with Leaflet GIS mapping featuring a custom dark-mode meteorological radar aesthetic.
    - Pre-mapped coordinates for all Philippine regions (NCR, Central Luzon, Bicol, Western Visayas,
      Eastern Visayas / Southern Leyte, Central Visayas, Northern Mindanao, BARMM, etc.).
  * Animated Doppler Heatmap Pulses:
    - Visualizes disaster intensity using concentric Doppler radar pulses that pulse dynamically
      according to calamity severity (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
  * Categorized Calamity Pins:
    - Distinct icons and color-coded map markers for Typhoons, Earthquakes, Flash Floods,
      Volcanic Activity, and Landslides.
  * Interactive Relief Dispatch Cards:
    - Clicking any regional radar pin reveals an interactive campaign overview card displaying:
      Campaign Title, Organizing NGO, Target Amount, Amount Raised, Urgency Level, and Beneficiary Impact.
    - Contains a direct "Donate Now" / "View Campaign" action button.
  * NGO Strategic Aid Intelligence:
    - Available inside both the Public Explorer and NGO Dashboard ("Disaster Relief Radar Heatmap" tab)
      allowing relief agencies to identify underserved calamity zones and mobilize resources efficiently.

3. NGO PUBLIC PROFILE & ACCREDITATION (NgoProfileModal)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Institutional NGO Transparency Profile & Public Credential Dossier

- Purpose:
  To eliminate donor skepticism, build authentic community trust, and completely deter fake or
  fly-by-night charities from exploiting disaster appeals.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * Comprehensive Transparency Modal (`NgoProfileModal.jsx`):
    Donors can click on any NGO name or badge across the platform to open a full institutional dossier:
    - Official Legal Name, Headquarters Address, Mission Statement, and Verified Hotlines.
    - Registered SEC Registration Number and DSWD Solicitation Permit Number.
    - Roster of the Board of Trustees and Executive Officers (e.g., Chairman, Secretary General).
    - Historical track record: Total active disaster campaigns deployed, cumulative funds raised
      in ETH and PHP, and verified beneficiary count.
    - Direct traditional payment credentials (verified GCash, PayMaya, and Bank Transfer details
      with official QR codes).
  * "Verified Philippine Relief Organization" Seal:
    Displays an authenticated green/blue verification badge indicating whether the entity was
    accredited by the AI Gemini Vision Engine or verified under Philippine SEC guidelines.
  * Direct Certificate Inspection:
    Includes an "Inspect Official SEC Certificate" button enabling donors to examine the official
    incorporation document in full resolution.

4. MULTI-CHANNEL DONATION TRACKING (HYBRID CRYPTO + FIAT)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Dual-Rail Disaster Donation Ledger (Ethereum Web3 + Philippine E-Wallets)

- Purpose:
  To bridge the gap between cutting-edge blockchain transparency and local Philippine payment
  accessibility, ensuring anyone can contribute whether they hold crypto or local e-wallets.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * Crypto Payment Rail (Web3 / Ethereum Sepolia):
    - Smart contract integration via Ethers.js and MetaMask / Web3 browsers.
    - Direct on-chain fund transfer to the campaign's smart contract address.
    - Real-time transaction hash generation, block confirmation tracking, and gas limit optimization.
  * Fiat Payment Rail (GCash, PayMaya, Bank Transfer):
    - High-resolution QR code display for instant scanning within Philippine banking and e-wallet apps.
    - Reference Number submission and validation to record fiat transactions into the relief system.
  * Unified Financial Progress Tracking:
    - Both cryptocurrency and fiat contributions are combined into a single real-time campaign
      progress bar with live PHP <-> ETH currency exchange rate calculations.
  * Donor Privacy & Receipts:
    - Donors can choose between "Public Recognition" or "Anonymous Donation" on every contribution.
    - Automatically generates verifiable digital donation receipts with transaction hashes and
      timestamped allocation breakdowns.

5. DONOR RECOGNITION BADGE SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Tiered Philanthropic Milestone & Recognition Badge Engine

- Purpose:
  To cultivate community engagement, incentivize sustained disaster aid participation, and honor
  generous benefactors who support vulnerable Philippine communities during emergencies.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * Cumulative Milestone Tiers:
    - First Responder: Awarded upon making a verified initial contribution to any active disaster appeal.
    - Community Lifeline / Guardian: Unlocked when cumulative donations cross intermediate impact
      milestones (e.g., ₱5,000+ or 0.05+ ETH).
    - Heroic Relief Champion: Conferred to patrons who contribute across multiple disaster relief campaigns.
    - Disaster Relief Pillar: Elite recognition for top contributors delivering sustained, major emergency aid.
  * Dynamic Profile & Leaderboard Showcase:
    - Badges are showcased with custom visual icons, metallic borders, and unlock dates inside the
      Donor Profile and public Donor Leaderboard.
  * Privacy-Preserving Recognition:
    - If a donor selects "Anonymous", their wallet address and email are masked from public view,
      while their contribution still counts toward platform milestones and campaign targets.

6. SEC ACCREDITATION, GEMINI AI VISION & ANTI-FRAUD ARCHITECTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Automated Institutional KYC with Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision & Universal Certificate Viewer

- Purpose:
  To provide 100% objective, automated, 3-second accreditation for legitimate NGOs while completely
  blocking fraudulent organizations, without manual administrative bottlenecks.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision Integration:
    - NGO uploads an SEC Certificate of Incorporation or DSWD Permit (PNG, JPG, or PDF).
    - Gemini AI Vision analyzes the document image, extracting the institutional header, Republic
      of the Philippines seal, legal organization title, and SEC Company Registration Number.
    - Instant Rubric Matching: Cross-references extracted document text against the user's input form.
      If it matches with high/medium confidence, the NGO is instantly AUTO-APPROVED with the auditor tag
      `AI_GEMINI_VISION`.
  * Anti-Fraud Autonomous Fallback Engine:
    - In case of external API rate limits, a secondary heuristic verification rubric evaluates document
      structure, registration regex patterns, and institutional credentials to prevent downtime.
  * Anti-Spam KYC Rate Limiter:
    - Enforces a strict maximum of 3 verification submissions per 24 hours per organization to prevent
      brute-force exploitation of the AI scanner.
  * Rejection Notice & Audit Transparency:
    - Rejected submissions receive detailed, constructive audit feedback (e.g., "Registration Number
      Mismatch: Document shows SEC-CN2021-08492, but input form entered SEC-FAKE-99999").
  * Universal Document Viewer (`SecCertificateModal.jsx`):
    - Solves broken image issues (`[x] SEC Certificate`) by normalizing SVG data URIs to RFC-compliant
      base64 strings (`normalizeSecDocUrl`).
    - Natively displays PDF documents in an embedded `<iframe />` viewer.
    - Certified Institutional Fallback: If an image is corrupt or blocked, renders a prestigious,
      gold-bordered Republic of the Philippines Certificate Card displaying verified credentials.
    - Integrated across Organization Portal, Admin Review Panel, and Donor Transparency Lightbox.

7. ENTERPRISE CLOUD DATABASE & RELIABLE EMAIL INFRASTRUCTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Feature Name:
  Distributed TiDB Cloud MySQL Migration & Multi-Hop Nodemailer Email Relay

- Purpose:
  To eliminate data loss between local development and live servers, and ensure 100% reliable OTP
  delivery for authentication and password recovery across all email domains.

- What It Does & How It Works:
  * TiDB Cloud Distributed MySQL Database:
    - Migrated backend persistence from ephemeral local SQLite/GitHub snapshots to a unified,
      high-availability TiDB Cloud Serverless MySQL cluster (`gateway01.ap-southeast-1.prod.aws.tidbcloud.com`).
    - Ensures that any donor account, NGO registration, campaign, or donation made on either localhost
      or the live production server (Render/Vercel) is permanently preserved and synchronized in real time.
    - Zero data wiped during Git commits or production redeployments.
  * Universal Email OTP Pipeline:
    - Configured direct Nodemailer Gmail SMTP with dedicated application credentials (`vlijrjrvwonjjmwe`).
    - Implemented an automatic fallback to Vercel Serverless Relay (`/api/send-email`) and Resend HTTP API
      to bypass cloud hosting SMTP port blocks (Render/AWS Port 587/465 restrictions).
    - Guaranteed OTP delivery for registration and password resets to all domains (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.).
